## What the accepted screens actually evaluate

All three families consume an already-closed, synchronized 15m candle, Features, Structure, Technical Regime, and MTF Regime. Their strategy returns `LONG_CANDIDATE`, `SHORT_CANDIDATE`, or `NO_TRADE`, not an order. The family-specific corpus generators evaluate every context, then use the existing Risk Manager, Operational Safety gate, sealed Decision Journal, and `EntryOrderIntentAuthorizer` only for a cleared candidate ([Trend corpus:461-503](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/research/trend_pullback_corpus.py), [Breakout corpus:390-432](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/research/breakout_retest_corpus.py), [Range corpus:439-472](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/research/range_mean_reversion_corpus.py), [intent:142-199](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/orders/intent.py)). Risk sizes from decision **reference close to structural invalidation**, not from the eventual fill ([risk/core.py:280-333](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/risk/core.py)). A candidate is never proof of admission, fill, or profit.

## Synthetic timeline and findings

### F1 — exact-boundary entry differs between shared simulator and research wrappers

**Expected before test:** for synthetic 15m opens `12:00=100`, `12:15=101`, `12:30=102`, decision and authorization at `12:15`, the simulator with source lower bound `12:15` and zero latency fills at `12:15`; with lower bound `12:15:00.000001` it fills at `12:30`. With +1s latency both select `12:30`. BUY and SELL select the same open (with opposite cost direction). **Actual:** exactly so in `test_source_lower_bound_and_latency_are_separate_entry_constraints` (`tests/backtest/test_execution.py`); command: `pytest -q tests/backtest/test_execution.py -k source_lower_bound`.

| Path / fixed case | Decision = authorization / request creation | Source lower bound | Eligible | Chosen open / fill time | Raw open |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shared simulator or Paper observation available at boundary; zero latency | 12:15 | 12:15 | 12:15 | 12:15 | 101 |
| All three research wrappers; zero latency | 12:15 | 12:15 + 1µs | 12:15 | 12:30 | 102 |
| Either source bound; +1s latency | 12:15 | as above | 12:15:01 | 12:30 | 102 |

The historical source filters **open times** in `[start,end)` ([backtest/execution.py:160-200,217-227](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/backtest/execution.py)); simulator selects first loaded open `>= created_at + latency` ([same:259-277](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/backtest/execution.py)). The +1µs bound in [Breakout:324-338](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/research/breakout_retest_replay.py), [Range:445-474](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/research/range_mean_reversion_replay.py), and [paired Trend:608-636](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/research/paired_exit_replay.py) therefore introduces one full 15m entry bar at zero latency, independent of configured latency. Shared fill pricing does not imply entry-timing parity. Paper accepts the first supplied observation at/after eligibility, with no historical source-open search ([paper/execution.py:247-315](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/paper/execution.py)); parity depends on observation availability and is not established by this audit. Original [#17](https://github.com/setup-co/adaptive-trading-bot/issues/17) explicitly permits the newly opening bar at an equal decision boundary. [#91](https://github.com/setup-co/adaptive-trading-bot/issues/91) likewise specifies entry through the first causally eligible simulator open; its separate current-context requirement is that **exit observation strictly follows entry fill**. The concrete additional **entry fill strictly after decision** condition is [PositionExitEvaluationContext._validate_fields():172-175](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/strategies/exit_core.py), `decision_at < entry_fill_at <= observed_at`, with equal-timestamp rejection in [`test_exit_context_enforces_strict_utc_and_entry_chronology`:119-129](../../tests/strategies/test_exit_core.py). The [paired replay comment:608-611](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/research/paired_exit_replay.py) excludes equal-timestamp entry liquidity to satisfy that exit-context contract; the other two wrappers also use +1µs. These are distinct constraints: `entry_fill > decision` is stronger than the simulator's eligibility rule, while `exit_observation > entry_fill` governs later exit evidence. Simply deleting the offset would leave a dependent exit-context validation conflict. **Classification: `DESIGN_DECISION_REQUIRED`**, not an assumed accidental typo: decide one causal, independently reviewed bar-boundary convention and its exit-context/accounting prerequisites before changing code. No alternative may use the just-closed candle's OHLC **as an execution fill reference** or use information unavailable at order creation; already observed closed-candle facts remain valid strategy/context inputs. Affected: all three accepted research entry paths and Backtest/Paper comparability; not a claim that changing timing improves returns.

### F2 — trigger is not an executable standing stop/target or guaranteed stop/R fill

**Expected before test:** synthetic LONG/SHORT low/high equality touch yields a trigger, dual touch selects protective stop, and a later gap-open market exit can fill beyond the stop. **Actual:** existing `test_fixed_2r_trigger_and_entry_boundary_cases`, `test_midpoint_and_stop_triggers_and_causal_execution`, and paired-stop tests plus the new synthetic `gap-stop` assertion pass (commands below). Breakout/RMR touch rules are [breakout:90-98,404-435](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/research/breakout_retest_replay.py) and [range:91-99,547-579](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/research/range_mean_reversion_replay.py). Trend paired uses LONG `low <= stop` / SHORT `high >= stop`, with stop precedence over simultaneous EMA close ([paired:861-918,1131-1136](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/research/paired_exit_replay.py)). The first evaluable completed candle may have opened at the actual entry-fill time; it cannot trigger an exit before its close ([breakout:372-403](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/research/breakout_retest_replay.py), [range:513-545](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/research/range_mean_reversion_replay.py)). At trigger close the runner creates one market request; first subsequent eligible open determines fill. With no eligible exit open, it records `exit_unfilled`/open position, not a forced close ([breakout:416-469](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/research/breakout_retest_replay.py)). In the fixed `gap-stop` fixture, stop basis is `901`, stop touches in candle 802, and next raw exit open `850`; actual exit fill is below 901. A target touch similarly does not promise +2R realized because exit costs and subsequent open govern it.

Paper is a separate observation-driven contract: coordinator authorizes a protective full close when an observed reference crosses the stop; the exit lifecycle fills only on a later supplied observation at/after eligibility, with common pricing ([paper/coordinator.py:613-711](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/paper/coordinator.py), [paper/exit_execution.py:163-241](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/paper/exit_execution.py)). Neither the historical completed-candle high/low test nor 2R/midpoint/EMA research policy is a standing venue stop or integrated Paper target. Original [#55](https://github.com/setup-co/adaptive-trading-bot/issues/55), [#57](https://github.com/setup-co/adaptive-trading-bot/issues/57), [#91](https://github.com/setup-co/adaptive-trading-bot/issues/91), [#147](https://github.com/setup-co/adaptive-trading-bot/issues/147), [#151](https://github.com/setup-co/adaptive-trading-bot/issues/151) specify these distinct boundaries. **Classification: `INTENTIONAL_MODEL_LIMITATION`**. Consequence: same-bar intrabar ordering, gap protection, MARK/LAST equivalence, standing-order fill and liquidation are unproven. Next action: document a separately approved protective/execution realism requirement before interpreting research exits as account-level protection; do not fabricate intrabar paths.

### F3 — invalid research admission retains a real simulated fill but no position economics

**Expected before test:** a costed entry fill on/wrong side of the sealed stop (or Range midpoint) yields `ENTRY_FILL_INVALIDATED` with entry order/fill and typed reason, but no research position, exit or PnL. **Actual:** `test_replay_actual_fill_geometry_and_unresolved_state`, `test_entry_fill_beyond_sealed_stop_is_explicitly_invalidated`, and Range geometry tests pass. Code returns before accounting ([breakout:352-367](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/research/breakout_retest_replay.py), [range:489-508](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/research/range_mean_reversion_replay.py), [paired:663-695](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/research/paired_exit_replay.py)); the Trend robustness population counts simulator-filled invalidations but excludes them from replayed closure/paired delta ([trend_pullback_exit_robustness.py:681-726](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/research/trend_pullback_exit_robustness.py)). This was explicitly approved as **research-only** in [#120](https://github.com/setup-co/adaptive-trading-bot/issues/120); Range additionally rejects a fill at/beyond the sealed midpoint per [#151](https://github.com/setup-co/adaptive-trading-bot/issues/151). **Classification: `DESIGN_DECISION_REQUIRED` for actual-account translation**, not a defect in the frozen research report. A real already-filled order cannot retroactively vanish; Paper currently accounts filled entries through its open-position lifecycle ([paper/coordinator.py:485-611](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/paper/coordinator.py)) and does not implement the research-only discard state. Next action: separately specify reconciliation/response to an invalidating *actual* fill; do not quietly equate conditional research exclusion to a zero-loss account event.

### F4 — temporary regime ineligibility resets both active and partial Range evidence

**Expected before test:** four uninterrupted LOW/HIGH/LOW/HIGH pivots at 20/40 produce `VALID_RANGE`; if the fourth newly confirmed pivot arrives during data-valid HTF-UP incompatibility **before this detector has formed a valid range**, snapshot `NOT_RANGE` with exactly `HTF_DIRECTIONAL`, usable partial history clears, but all four seen identities remain. Return to compatible context with no new pivot gives `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` / `NEED_FOUR_CONFIRMED_PIVOTS`; replaying the fourth identity raises duplicate atomically. **Actual:** `test_temporary_directional_context_clears_pivots_but_retains_seen_identities` passes. The detector stages newly confirmed identities *before* compatibility, then its unconditional compatibility branch clears usable history and active range while retaining seen IDs ([range_context.py:206-238,277-361](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/strategies/range_context.py)). Separately, [#31](https://github.com/setup-co/adaptive-trading-bot/issues/31) explicitly requires clearing when a **previously validated range** encounters incompatibility; `test_incompatible_context_invalidates_and_clears_history` builds a valid range first and verifies that case. Neither that test nor #31's conditional wording establishes that clearing **partial pre-validation history** was explicitly required. Its broader design intent remains unproven by those sources; this is not automatically a bug. A valid active range becomes `RANGE_STALE` after age 16 and clears; close beyond breakout threshold yields `RANGE_BROKEN` and clears; invalid chronology/data raises without committing ([range_context.py:209-216,240-268,373-410](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/strategies/range_context.py), existing range-context tests). **Classification: `DESIGN_DECISION_REQUIRED`** for the partial-history reset; already-validated-range invalidation is a specified contract. Affected: Range candidate availability, not Risk/Safety. Minimal decision: explicitly decide whether previously seen but not yet qualifying pivots may survive *only temporary, data-valid* regime ineligibility; keep stale/breakout/malformed invalidation distinct, and predeclare a new causal test/protocol before any historical comparison. #161's overlapping reason occurrences and zero candidates do **not** prove this reset caused scarcity.

### F5 — shared costs are applied per fill; approved-risk R is not realized stop loss

**Expected before test:** at 2 bps full spread, 2 bps slippage, 10 bps fee, half-spread is 1 bp, so BUY raw 100 becomes 100.03 and SELL raw 100 becomes 99.97. Fees use each *actual fill notional*, not one average notional. **Actual:** `test_two_two_ten_bps_gap_exit_keeps_actual_fill_and_fee_arithmetic` passes through `BacktestExecutionSimulator` and `PositionAccountingEngine` (source authority [simulation.py:89-99](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/execution/simulation.py), [accounting/positions.py:295-325](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/accounting/positions.py)). For quantity 1 and LONG raw exit 90: entry 100.03, exit 89.973, gross -10.057, entry/exit fees 0.10003 + 0.089973 = 0.190003, net -10.247003. SHORT raw exit 110: entry 99.97, cover 110.033, gross -10.063, fees 0.09997 + 0.110033 = 0.210003, net -10.273003. These fixed examples are synthetic, **not** observed WEEX fees or historical performance. The approximate 3 bp adverse price adjustment *per fill* plus 10 bp fee *per fill* is not an exact 26 bp round-trip currency amount when entry/exit notionals differ.

Risk's stop distance is decision reference-to-invalidation and approved risk is `approved_quantity * stop_distance`, capped by budget and exposure ([risk/core.py:280-333,387-410](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/risk/core.py); [#35](https://github.com/setup-co/adaptive-trading-bot/issues/35)). A separate illustrative authorized quantity 1, reference 100, stop 95 would have nominal stop distance and approved risk 5; the above LONG net loss would be -2.0494006 approved-risk R **if** those were its sealed Risk facts. It is not a claim that the test order was Risk-authorized. The actual screen denominator is precisely the sealed intent's `approved_risk_quote`, not recomputed from actual fill ([trend_pullback_signal_edge.py:139-164,248-264](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/research/trend_pullback_signal_edge.py); [#144](https://github.com/setup-co/adaptive-trading-bot/issues/144)); nominal stop risk, approved risk, and realized loss need not coincide. Existing accepted report outcomes preserve net PnL/R, fees and population; they do not separately expose a per-trade spread/slippage decomposition or prove current WEEX fee tiers. **Classification: `NOT_REPRODUCED`** for suspected omitted costs/R arithmetic; **`INTENTIONAL_MODEL_LIMITATION`** for assumed costs and missing venue-specific observability. Next action: if material for the next gate, predeclare an audit-only cost-attribution view from existing authoritative fill facts, not a new pricing or accounting formula.

Finally, current Risk defaults are controlled-testing unleveraged caps (`risk_per_trade_fraction=.0025`, simultaneous `.005`, gross exposure `<=1`; [risk/core.py:58-66](../../src/adaptive_trading_bot/risk/core.py)). Master Plan v1.3 requires separately validated Risk-owned leverage, margin, funding, MARK/liquidation and account-survival realism before representative leveraged readiness. The absence of those mechanics in these *explicitly unleveraged* screens is a readiness gap, not proof that current Risk violated its own declared test contract. **Classification: `INTENTIONAL_MODEL_LIMITATION`**; no leverage or risk-limit change is authorized here.
